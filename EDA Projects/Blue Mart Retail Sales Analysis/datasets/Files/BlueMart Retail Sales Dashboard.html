Insights and recommendations

1. Sales follow the seasons, and they are not growing

January and February are the peak (about $2.0M and $1.8M a month). September to November are the slowest ($0.75M–$0.82M). December is also weak ($1.07M), so it is not a peak. Yearly sales stayed between $14.6M and $15.3M from 2021 to 2024.

Do: Plan stock and staff for Jan–Feb. Run offers in Sep–Nov. Do not over-order for December.

2. 2025 is behind 2024

For January to October, 2025 made $13.06M and 2024 made $13.52M. That is 3.4% lower. The spring dip moved: March rose to $1.58M, but April fell to $0.74M (it was $1.20M in 2024).

Do: Check which channels and categories fell in 2025. Study April 2025 first.

3. Electronics is more than half of the business

Electronics gives 55.7% of sales and 56% of profit. Batteries, cables, chargers and mobile accessories make almost all of that profit ($11.8M). Margins are close in every category (about 29%), except Personal Care (25.9%).

Do: Protect Electronics supply and price. Sell bundles (charger + cable + battery). Review Personal Care prices and costs.

4. City does not drive sales. Store count does.

Every city sells about $1.46M per store. Dubai has 56% of sales because it has 56% of stores. So Abu Dhabi and Sharjah are not weaker. Each store earns about $0.3M a year.

Do: Grow by opening stores or adding services, in any city. Learn from the top stores, but expect small gains: all stores are within about 2.5% of each other.

5. Online is bigger than stores

Stores give 41.3% of sales. Online gives 58.7%: website 27.7%, mobile app 17.9%, Amazon.ae and Noon 13.1%. The website sells 1.5 times more than the app.

Do: Improve the app (offers, notifications). Check the fees on Amazon.ae and Noon. Next step: compare profit margin by channel.

6. Three popular products earn too little

Rice (SKU 1121) has a 14.4% margin. Paper Products (1028) has 18.1%. Soap (1156) has 19.2%. The average is 28.8%.

Do: Raise the price or cut the cost. Reaching a 25% margin would add about $65K over the 58 months. It is small but easy.
